Waiver Wire Week Five: Tesla Roadster
We are adding the new Tesla Roadster from the Waiver Wire to the starting lineup this week.

Standing at a stout four feet flat and weighing at a gobstopping 3,700 pounds (equivalent to eleven prime Vince Wilforks). This thing is in a class of its own.

Not only does it have a zero to sixty time at 0.59 seconds (equivalent to a 1.5 second forty yard dash), but the car can do a fucking tailwhip in the air.

Tesla demonstrated this flying (presumably street legal) car in Waco, Texas (The coolest city in the world). If I were a trillionaire I would be doing the same shit. On the Bing search engine I searched “google” which brought me to google, then I googled “how much money would it cost to end world hunger?” Google said 93 billion dollars.

I don’t know if I am alone here, but I would rather have really cool Tesla Roadsters flying at the speed of light through my local school zone, then have children with bellies full of food.

When this car gets released to the public… I uh… I can’t comprehend what will happen. I’m assuming mostly good things.

Musk was quoted after the Baptist preachers in Waco saw the car drive up a ramp and do a triple aerial, saying that he didn’t want people in the car during the demonstration “to make sure we don’t kill anyone.”

Nice! Safety is always the first priority.
Judge A Book By Its Cover
Rating: 10/10
I dropped my phone while trying to impress my nephew the other day. I was doing trey flips with my phone, and my nephew kept yelling higher! HIGHER! So I was throwing it higher and higher, and right as my phone reached a climax of twenty feet in the air, I did a 360 jump with a jazz hand finish. I looked up to find the phone, but I lost it in the sun, and it came crashing down on my cement patio. I don’t have a phone case because I like to live on the edge, so my phone cracked into a million pieces.

Don’t worry. I blamed it on the little bastard to my mom and made him cry.

Eat it kid.

Having a cracked phone makes it very dangerous to scroll on reels, so I had to get the screen replaced.

This led me to not have a phone for four hours this Tuesday. This also coincided with a dentist appointment that I forgot I had after I put my phone in the hands of the seventeen year old with a receding hairline at UbreakIfix.

This was gearing up to be the most frightening experience of my entire life.

Being in a waiting room at the dentist, knowing I haven’t flossed in six years and no phone is literally worse than getting life for a crime you didn’t commit.

This led me to go insane for about five minutes, and then I found out magazines existed in the dentist waiting room.

My family dentist is a struggling business so these magazines haven’t been updated since the Obama administration, but this led me to see some interesting Magazine covers.

I was perusing around the opulent early 2000s Southern Comfort magazines and then I saw it.

The May 2011 edition of People magazine: Country Special: Keith Urban Sexy New Photos!: Country’s Hottest Guys!: 50 Nashville Men We Love + Fast Family Meals!

I had never seen anything like it. This Magazine cover, reset my dopamine chambers and produced an adrenaline rush like no other. I forgot I didn’t have a phone for like five minutes.

I even broke my number one life rule: reading, and read (kinda) the entire magazine.

I did the thing where you fake super read something by riffling through the pages and shaking your head violently side to side.

“DONE!” I screamed. Slamming the magazine on the floor. I did it. I read something (kinda).

It turns out I have eight cavities.

10/10 The first time a cover made me read (kinda)
This Is Off The Dome But getting a reaction’s on a text in the group chat feels better than a hug from your mother.

Is there anything better than a hug from your mother?

It’s the single thing that connects a boy to his mother. Years and years of unconditional love coiled in bounds of blood interlocking this wondrous relationship. Everyone’s mother has known them for longer than they can remember I’m pretty sure.

My first memory is most likely a hug from my mother. Damn, that’s crazy.

A single warm embrace that goes through every generation of mothers and their sons/daughters but mostly sons.

In my brain I am currently thinking of the best hug a mother can give a son.

Let me paint you a picture.

My mother is at a Dallas Wings basketball game, and she got randomly selected to do the halftime half-court shot for $10,000 sponsored by HEB.

She’s nervous. She can’t shoot a basketball to save her life. She’s gonna make a fool of herself in front of the packed out Dallas Wing’s Arena that can hold a capacity of 6,251 people.

She also hasn’t seen her son (Me. In this scenario in my head) since he left for the Army.

I have been gone for six years in Iraq. I write to her when I can.

She gears up to shoot the halfcourt shot. She dribbles once, twice, three times. Then out of the speakers plays “Coming Home” by Diddy.

Wow I did not know Diddy made that song. Okay… imagination continue:

“I’m comin’ home, I’m comin’ home
Tell the world that i’m comin’ home”

My mom is like,

Why is this song playing? They should be playing the 1984 hit “Basketball” by Kurtis Blow!

Right before she shoots, gasps and a roaring cheer arises from the crowd…

But, I haven’t shot yet? My mom is thinking. Why is the crowd cheering?

Little does she know that there are actually 6,252 people at this game. I (me) got dishonorably discharged because I quit from being too scared of war (I’m a lover not a fighter). No one knows this but me, and I lied to the Dallas Wings organization about why I came home early.

She feels a tap on her shoulder, she turns around to see me in full uniform (Technically stolen valor at this point) holding a bouquet of Hydrangeas, her favorite flower. Tears well up in my eyes.

“I’m home mah.”

We embrace in a hug that feels like a thousand hugs in one. Six years away from my mother, not knowing if I would ever come back. Through the ups and the downs. The trials and tribulations of having a child and caring for that child every single day. That child chose to serve this country (technically at one point). She is my momma bear and I am her cub. Dopamine and serotonin course through our veins through this hug. It’s unexplainable.

It is a hug from your mother.

Wow. I made myself cry with this made up story about a mother’s hug, but there is actually something better than a hug from your mother.

A couple days ago I sent a text to my group chat of my “friends” that do not hold a candle to the connection I have with my mother.

They gave me six “haha” reactions to the message I sent.

Six.

I didn’t even know what to do at that moment. Pure elation. A type of happiness that nothing can ever touch. I found nirvana.

The text message didn’t even come from my brain. It was a screen shot of an A.I. description of the very group chat everyone was in.

“Mini Kiss a year ago; watching football alone; speculation about Justin Herbert’s health.”

Pure comedic genius. This text has everything. Reminiscing about the past and comparing it to the melancholic present, “speculation about Justin Herbert’s health” thrown in as a Kawhi Leonard quadruple bounce buzzerbeater.

The public loved it. Instant gratification. Put these six “haha” reactions in an emptied out swisher sweet and smoke it. Pure jubilation.

A hug from my mother doesn’t sniff six “haha” reactions from guys that I met four years ago.

Sorry mom, you just don’t get it.

But let me ask you this.

Are you a coveted member of Forbes 30 under 30?
Did Forbes give you a Self-Made score of 8/10?
Are you 42 years young with your full life ahead of you?

So what, she’s technically worth zero dollars. You have to start from somewhere. Every self-made billionaire has started from zero, she’s done it once, so she can do it again.

Her life is so cool that they made a real life documentary about it.

If I were to switch lives with anyone it would still be Julian Newman, but the close second is of course Elizabeth Holmes. We’re talking about the Mount Rushmore of greatest humans of all time here!

Let’s all give Elizabeth Holmes some flowers because she deserves it.
