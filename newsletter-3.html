Waiver Wire Week Three: Chet Holmgren
Standing at a staggering 7’ 1” going from 213 lbs to 215 lbs this off season, he would be the perfect tight end to get off of waivers.

Gaining two pounds over the off season is true grit from the Thunder forward. This height and weight ratio is perfect for the NFL.

As someone who is 6 feet tall (6’ 6” while wearing my mom’s stilettos) this would be the equivalent of me weighing 154 lbs. I would be shredded.

That means Chet is shredded AND 7 feet tall. Throw the guy a goal line fade and that 50/50 ball turns into 100 Chet.

Chet is a year older than me and he is entering his fifth year in the NBA, and I am three weeks into this sick newsletter thing. Who’s the real winner in life?

Don’t answer that.

This Is Off The Dome But, a childhood sleepover was the last time I was truly happy

Let me paint you a picture. You’re in fifth grade, autumn has just begun, you’ve only really seen like six autumns so you’re taking this perfect weather for granted. It’s Friday, school just ended. You lowkey fumbled a public side hug to your girlfriend of three days at recess, but you’re breaking up with her in three days so it doesn’t matter. You go home for like five minutes to see a packed bag on your bed, and your mom tells you that you’re going to a sleepover at your boy’s crib that is slightly nicer than yours, and they have a trampoline AND a game room.

I am tearing up while typing this. This was literally the last time I was happy, and technically the last time I was sober hanging out with friends. That still doesn’t correlate so make sure to drink this weekend.

I can’t even comprehend with words how good a sleepover felt as a nine year old.

But I’ll try.

1.It’s like going through puberty and realizing you’re going to be over six feet tall, so now your dad can finally respect you as a man.
2.It’s like bringing your ukulele to a party and playing Riptide by Vance Joy to your crush, and it didn’t work, but a music scout was there and he signed you to a record label.
3.It’s like our founding fathers combining a forty ounce beer and a green apple four loko and calling it a sidewalk slammer.

That is what a sleepover feels like.

No rules applied at sleepovers. You would play Kick The Can, and sweat way more than you were anticipating, then you would see your girlfriend of three days, absolutely pissed because you fucked up the public side hug. You break up with her, and then get back together thirty minutes later because she saw you run kinda fast. The main activity of the night would just be to stay up the whole night, only using sugar to stay awake. And if you do fall asleep, you would wake up in the most public part of the house, and hear how this middle class family operates in the morning. You fake being asleep until they notice you on the couch using the coffee table as a blanket. You also threw up neon orange fizz all over their bathroom floor because you had one too many sunkists, but you don’t tell a soul, they’ll find out.

You eat a donut, and realize it’s Saturday so you don’t have to go to Church with this random family. Thank you Jesus.

GOD, I MISS THIS SHIT. I’M CRYING RIGHT NOW. REALLY SAD TEARS.

You would go back to your house the next day literally hungover from sugar (which is technically more addicting than cocaine) from the night before, and gossip with your sisters (everyone reading this has two older sisters that they would gossip to) about your girlfriend troubles.

And you would do it again the next night.

Coming back to school that next Monday felt like a fever dream. You put on your neon outfit with your cleanest pair of Nike Roshes. It’s 7am and forty degrees outside. You’re wearing shorts, but it’s okay because you pull your Nike elite’s up to your knees to make sweatpants.

You fake a headache during class, go to the nurse, tell them it’s a migraine so they think that this is a serious issue, and they send you home. Your mom has to take time off from her job to take care of you because you can’t handle the stress of music class. White-crossed-buns is way too hard to play on the recorder when you’re hungover.

We need to bring back sleepovers. Pure sober chaos.

But

If I spent my entire life wanting to work for the Irkutsk Anti-Plague Institute of Siberia and the Far East, the number one rule would probably be, don’t drop the tube containing the black plague.

What are the Russians even doing with the Black Death? Sick nickname for a plague by the way.

If I am Mr. Beast though. I just got a sick new video idea. He should get into the infectious disease territory. “Last To Take Hand Off Plague Gets Half Of Their Student Loans Paid Off!”

If you work at the anti-plague place, and you drop the plague, my guess would be that you are fired, but I’m pretty sure she died from the plague that she dropped, so I guess that solves that question.

That is a hard job. Living in Irkutsk, Siberia, holding the black plague for a living.

I can’t relate, but another COVID/Black Death would be sick.
