Waiver Wire Week Four: Mark Zuckerberg
We are adding Mark Zuckerberg to the lineup this week. Standing at a brisk five feet seven inches, and weighing a buck fifty-four he quite honestly does not have a place on a football field.

But he has a place in my heart.

I do not follow this man on any of my socials, but he showed up on my feed today smoking meat and taking a bite out of a watermelon.

I can’t explain what this photo does to me. The stance is so powerful. He looks like George Washington crossing the Delaware leading his men to the new revolution.

In the background of this photo he has a slackline tied to a tree. I can imagine him trying to balance, the slack is shaking beneath his feet, his legs gyrating side to side trying to keep balance. Zuck walks across to the other side. He jumps off the slackline doing a C-jump in celebration.

This waiver wire has nothing to do with fantasy football, but I just want to add this vibe to my rotation this week.
Judge A Book By Its Cover
Rating: 5/10
My mother recommended I read this book after I told her I go on morning walks, and occasionally drink chamomile tea.

I bought the book via Amazon, the website. I still haven’t read a page, but I’ve heard this book has been adapted for the screen.

I have always wanted to pull a “The book is better than the movie/tv show.” Because it makes me sound smart. I have never done it before.

I just found out that Harry Potter was based on J.K. Rowling’s fictional series “Harry Potter”

I looked like a fool while being in a screaming match with my ten year old niece because I thought there was no way Harry Potter could have been a book of fiction. My dumbass thought Harry Potter was a found footage documentary about what life is like in England.

This led me down a rabbit hole finding out what the word ‘fiction’ actually meant. Am I the only one that believes everything they read is real?

This book cover is pretty cool though. I like the hockey players doing a face off. I can imagine they play on rival teams or something, hence the name. I like the colors, and the symmetry. The Author’s name in red works as a power bottom at the end of the cover.

This looks like the type of book I’d bring out to a public setting and read with my legs crossed, because I like the pressure on my upper thigh.

5/10 I can work with 5, but i’m always looking for more
This Is Off The Dome But I HATE Liberty Mutual commercials. Especially Liberty Biberty…

Sunday, the day of the sabbath. My day of rest.

My weekend ends with a restart of the mind. I wake up from a ten hour slumber in my silk pajamas, and I immediately take a walk outside with no distractions other than my phone. This is my weekly five minutes of outdoor time where I think about things.

Things including:

1.The awesome decisions I made over the weekend that leave me with no regret whatsoever
3.I think about how beautiful the colors changing on the autumnal leaves are, but that beauty actually just shows the slow, unchanging death that will soon, at some point in everyone’s life, come to all of us.
2.I think about the next twelve hours I have to myself. Where I can pause the slow, unchanging, death thoughts and watch football on the television.

I retrieve from my five minute outdoor prison sentence to sit on my chaise lounge like a medieval king. I then watch modern day grid-iron gladiators forcefully give each other degenerative brain disease as I sit, point, and laugh whilst eating a Costco rotisserie chicken only using my hands as utensils.

I think of myself as a modern day King Joffrey watching football. Mostly because I look like a twelve year old after a clean shave, and nobody likes me. I’m still the King though, and nothing bad will ever happen to me. (I just started season four of Game of Thrones, please no one tell me what happens.)

A Sunday full of football. I love it. It’s mine. I created this life where I’m able to rest and relax my brain.

Then out the corner of my eye. I see an animatronic ball of yellow fuzz enter my football filled screen.

What is this? The football game has turned into a musical trying to sell me life insurance.

Liberty Mutual. Liberty FUCKING BIBERTY. My day cosplaying as King Joffrey is completely ruined!

As a football fan, each year we are subjected to thirty seconds of constant torture seeing whatever fucked up thing Liberty Mutual decides to put on our quad box.

I want to put Biberty between my really strong thighs and squeeze him like an oversized watermelon.

I want to see the life in his fake googly eyes slowly fade away.

What even is life insurance anyway? I had no idea, so I mustered up the courage and called my dad. He became disappointed in me. The last time I called him I wished him a happy birthday four days after his actual birthday. This happened five years ago.

Whoops.

Anyways, my dad told me you buy life insurance, so when you die, other people get money.

That’s kind of sad.

Now I’m thinking about slow, unchanging death again. When I think about death I want to watch football, but I look at my football screen device and it’s showing a yellow fuzz ball with googly eyes telling me to buy a monthly subscription for death. I am in a cycle of death. This is not good.

If I can’t escape it, I might as well buy in.

Liberty Mutual needs to stop soft launching buying a death subscription using a Liberty Biberty or Doug and his Emu. They need to be upfront about their marketing strategy.

I did some digging, trying to find sporting events or events in general that need sponsors, because everything needs a sponsor, and I don’t know if you read the news on reels, but I found out that the U.S. is livestreaming a public execution sometime in the future.

Yikes…

They probably have like zero sponsors for that.

Well, Liberty Mutual, if you hire me then I will help get you in on that. I can already see it, the headline will read:

Public Execution of Nidal Hasan, murderer of 13 unarmed men, brought to you by Liberty Mutual and Liquid Death Spring Water.

God it’s perfect, phenomenal even, and you know what they say, all press is good press.

This has me feeling like King Joffrey again, and I don’t think this is a good thing.
Fark of the week: The Colorado football coach that left the Offensive playbook at the hotel bar

As someone who loves a good hotel bar, I can totally see this happening to me if I ever was a football coach.

Also,

as a performative man myself,

Who always finds himself reading feminist literature at Clairo concerts whilst drinking a lavender matcha whilst listening to Ruth Bader Ginsburg court hearings with wired headphones connected to a portable vinyl recording set that sits promptly in my over the shoulder MoMA giftshop totebag.

I can totally see a performative football coach trying to stand out at the bar reading the Colorado offensive playbook whilst sipping on a bud heavy listening to Lynyrd Skynyrd out loud on his Turtlebox speaker that he brought to the hotel bar.

We get it dude, you coach football. Save some for the rest of us.

Anyways. He did fark this one up. Leaving an offensive football playbook at the bar, and the other team gets a hold of it, and your team ends up losing the game because of the offensive play.

You’re getting canned.

But hey, at least you looked pretty cool at the bar for like forty-five minutes.
Venus Williams
Venus Fun Fact: Venus Williams’ karaoke song of choice in Gold Dust Woman by Fleetwood Mac.
My dream is to be seven dirty shirleys deep in a run down bar in Guangdong, China. I hear Gold Dust Woman start up, and I look to see Venus, absolutely singing her little heart out.
We lock eyes and just stare for what it seems to be hours but it’s only mere seconds. Then I get up there to do karaoke and act like I can’t sing. I shouldn’t be up there, but my friend John McEnroe tells me,
“C’mon Teorge! Get your ass up there and sing for your best friend!”
I start.
I kill it.
I sing Oasis’ Don’t Look Back In Anger. At the end of the song the whole bar is swaying side to side with their phone flashlights on, creating a scene unlike any other. Liam Gallagher joins me on stage to sing the final chorus.
Liam and I dap up and nuzzle our noses. He lets me join the band as the new bass guitarist. I tell him,
“But Liam, I don’t know how to play the bass guitar.”
Liam responds,
“Who cares, I just like your vibe, and also, no one actually knows how to play bass guitar, they just stand there, flick their fingers and look cool.”
A single tear starts to slowly fall down my cheek.
Then I wake up from my dream.
Fuck.
